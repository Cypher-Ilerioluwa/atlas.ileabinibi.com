About the content

The Atlas is in a pilot stage. Entries carry evidence labels, but they may be incomplete or disputed, and some are demonstrations that have not been reviewed. Please treat the Atlas as a starting point and check important facts with your elders and other sources. Tell us about mistakes through the corrections policy.
