How the Kọ́jọ́dá calendar works

The year has 13 months of 28 days, which is 364 days. Each month has 7 weeks of 4 days, so a year has 91 weeks. The first day of the year is 3 June.

A Gregorian year is one or two days longer than 364. Those extra days are not given numbers: they stay on the last day of the year, as in the original Kọ́jọ́dá calendar.

A cycle is named here by the Gregorian year in which it starts (for example, the cycle that began on 3 June 2026). The original calendar does not number years.
