About Ilẹ̀ Abínibí Atlas

The Atlas is a companion to ileabinibi.com: a place to find Abínibí communities, kingdoms and diaspora sites, and to see what each claim rests on.

Purpose

The Atlas aims to document and connect Abínibí communities, landmarks, festivals, people and traditions across Nigeria, West Africa and the diaspora. It is in a pilot stage, so many planned sections are not built yet.

How evidence is labelled

Every entry carries an evidence label so readers can tell sources of different kinds apart.

  • Documented supported by written or recorded sources.
  • Oral tradition held in community memory and testimony.
  • Scholarly interpretation a researcher’s reading of the evidence.

Disputed accounts, community submissions and entries under review will be added as separate labels. Where accounts differ, the Atlas will present them side by side instead of choosing one.

Current data

The places on the map are demonstration entries carried over from the first prototype. They have not been editorially reviewed, and coordinates are approximate city centres rather than boundaries. They will be replaced by reviewed records.

The Kọ́jọ́dá calendar

The Atlas will reuse the rules of the existing Kọ́jọ́dá calendar at kojoda.netlify.app. Kọ́jọ́dá dates appear only after the conversion has been checked against that calendar.

Privacy and cultural sensitivity

Sacred, restricted and private information will not be published merely because it was submitted. Corrections can be sent through the corrections policy of Ilẹ̀ Abínibí.
