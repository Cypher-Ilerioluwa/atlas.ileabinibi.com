Add to Home Screen

Install the Atlas on your iPhone or iPad. Apple does not allow websites to do this automatically, so it takes three taps:

  1. 1
    Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. 3
    Tap Add. The Atlas now opens like an app

Not seeing it? Open this page in Safari first. Notifications on iPhone work after installing.
