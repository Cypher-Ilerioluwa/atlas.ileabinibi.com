Privacy

What we collect, who can see it and the choices you have, in plain language.

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

The Ilẹ̀ Abínibí Atlas (atlas.ileabinibi.com) is run by Ilẹ̀ Abínibí, the team behind ileabinibi.com. In this page, “we” means that team.

What we collect

If you only read the site, we do not ask for any personal information. Our hosting provider keeps ordinary technical logs (such as your IP address and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

If you create an account, we store:

  • your email address and a display name, and a profile picture if you add one;
  • what you add or do on the site: articles, events and corrections you submit, comments, likes, saved items, chat messages and photos, and music links you share;
  • your notification choices and, if you turn on push notifications, the address your browser or phone gives us for sending them.

What is public

Your display name, picture, comments, chat messages, shared photos and shared music links can be seen by other visitors, and chat can be read without signing in. Please do not share private information such as phone numbers, home addresses or details about other people.

What is kept on your device

The Atlas saves small items in your browser: your sign-in session, light or dark theme, whether you have seen a tip, your daily quiz progress, and a copy of pages so they open faster and work offline. The Atlas does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Other services that receive some data

  • Supabase stores accounts and content and handles sign-in.
  • Netlify hosts the site and sends push notifications on our behalf.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so Google can see your IP address when a page loads.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map tiles.
  • Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, SoundCloud and similar services load only when you press Play on shared music, and they have their own privacy policies.
  • Browser push services (run by Google, Apple or Mozilla) deliver notifications to your device.

How we use it

We use your information to run your account, show your contributions, send the notifications you ask for, keep the community safe and improve the Atlas. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices and rights

You can change your profile, switch notifications on or off in Settings, and turn push off in your browser at any time. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and similar laws you may ask us to access, correct or delete your information, to stop processing it, or to withdraw consent. Contact us using the details on the Contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account we remove your personal details; public contributions may be removed or shown without your name. Backups are cleared on a regular cycle.

Children

The Atlas is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Young people should use the Atlas with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to it is limited by database rules. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date above, and tell signed-in members when a change matters.
